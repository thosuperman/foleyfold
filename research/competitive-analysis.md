# Competitive evidence
Desk research, Nov 22 2026. Product descriptions show overlap, not exhaustive proof of absence. Limitations below refer to the documented workflow, not benchmarked defects.

| Finalist | Related work and where it stops | Opportunity / verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Sound caption cutting room | [Subtitle Edit](https://github.com/SubtitleEdit/subtitleedit/) is a broad subtitle editor; [Amara](https://amara.org/subtitling-platform/) centers hosted media and team authoring; [Soundscape](https://devpost.com/software/sound-thing) automatically classifies sounds in uploaded files and overlays results. | Do not compete on transcription or claim sound recognition. Let a human spot the sound, draft only from their note on-device, compare against actual timed playback, explicitly permit note-only cloud fallback, export human-approved WebVTT. Achievable without training a classifier. |
| Actor cue rehearsal | [Script Rehearser](https://www.scriptrehearser.com/) imports scripts and plays recordings; [coldRead](https://www.coldreadapp.com/) follows spoken cues; [LineLearner](https://www.peterallday.com/linelearner/) rehearses recorded dialogue. | Private line-intention drafts add little beyond familiar role and playback tools; convincing cue listening would require more than the web hybrid model. |
| Sensory museum itinerary | [Smithsonian](https://access.si.edu/pre-visit-materials) publishes venue-specific sensory information; [British Museum](https://www.britishmuseum.org/visit/accessibility-museum) provides visitor accessibility resources; [Peabody Essex Museum](https://www.pem.org/visit/accessibility) offers pre-visit planning. | Private preference-aware alternatives are useful, but trustworthy plans need maintained venue evidence; invented accessibility advice would harm trust. |

[W3C](https://www.w3.org/WAI/media/av/captions/) distinguishes dialogue and meaningful non-speech audio, and notes editorial judgement is needed. [Berry, *Reading and listening to Foley and captions*](https://doi.org/10.1386/ts_00032_1) connects captions to sonic storytelling. These support the problem, not proof our wording is accessible to every viewer. Further discovery: [Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=43800251), [Product Hunt captioning tools](https://www.producthunt.com/products/captioncreator-cc), app listings via the rehearsal products, and Reddit search (no reliable primary source used). No market-size or first-ever claim.

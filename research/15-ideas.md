# Fifteen distinct concepts

1. **Sound caption cutting room.** Indie editors turn heard cues into timed, reviewable sound captions without sharing rushes.
2. **Actor cue rehearsal.** Actors rehearse hidden lines and alternative intentions backstage.
3. **Sensory itinerary.** Museum visitors compose private routes from trusted sensory guides.
4. **Quilt contrast proof.** Quilters test color order and get a verbal contrast critique from swatches.
5. **Conversation repair deck.** AAC users build personal clarification phrases for noisy places.
6. **Puzzle clue referee.** Constructors check ambiguous clues without disclosing unpublished grids.
7. **Type specimen casting.** Graphic designers audition synthetic microcopy against real glyph widths.
8. **Shared kitchen labels.** Households draft legible prep labels with human-reviewed translations.
9. **Oral history release editor.** Archivists review consent boundaries in short excerpts.
10. **Lyric stress score.** Songwriters test singable rewrites against stress grids offline.
11. **Touring prop ledger.** Stagehands turn margin cues into a backstage shift sequence.
12. **Plant dye swatchbook.** Dyers name batches and compare color notes at the workbench.
13. **Board game teach-back.** Players rehearse teaching one round using their own rule excerpts.
14. **Grief ritual printer.** Families compose private small keepsake cards offline.
15. **Gallery tactile label.** Exhibit designers draft tactile exploration prompts from curator notes.

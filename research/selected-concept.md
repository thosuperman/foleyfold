# Foleyfold
Foley (performed sound) + fold (condensing sound into a readable line). Exact-name web and GitHub repository searches on Nov 22 did not surface an obvious captioning product; this is not trademark clearance.

Independent editors with an unreleased cut need the story behind a bell, a hiss, a receding rhythm. Dialogue transcription alone misses it. A person listens and marks a time span, describes it, drafts wording with the hybrid model if available and reviews it against the picture. The output goes directly into another editor as WebVTT.

We selected this over actor rehearsal and museum planning after the competitor and feasibility pass: a single text prompt fits the documented on-device capability. The model never has to identify audio or infer disabilities. Timing, preview and export are useful even without inference. Unlike Rounds, this is post-production artistry, not field inspection. Most important unvalidated assumption: Deaf/hard-of-hearing reviewers and editors find these human-reviewed sound descriptions useful. Arrange a co-design review rather than claim validation.

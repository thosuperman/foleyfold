# Rules and release gates
Read 22 November 2026: [official rules §§3–9](https://firebasehybridaihackathon.devpost.com/rules), [overview](https://firebasehybridaihackathon.devpost.com/) and [resources](https://firebasehybridaihackathon.devpost.com/resources). Rules are authoritative; confirm changes before entering.

- Window: 11 Nov 13:30 PT through **30 Nov 2026 14:00 PST = 22:00 UTC** (1 Dec 07:00 JST). Judging Dec 10–16; keep test access free throughout. Announcement expected Dec 21.
- Eligibility: legal majority (Taiwan ≥20), internet access as of Nov 11, export controls, geographical, government employment/conflict and sponsor/administrator affiliation exclusions. See §3 for the full lists and terms. We cannot verify a person's eligibility.
- New work or substantial updates during the window; multiple entries permitted only if substantially different. Rounds is field inspection; this is a filmmaker's editing tool. AI coding assistants are allowed; disclose pre-existing code/work. Owner must confirm original work, rights, eligibility and role. No claim that using an agent guarantees eligibility.
- Required: functional English app, runtime Firebase AI Logic integration, App Check (debug tokens accepted), supported platform: Chrome web/extension with Gemini Nano 4, Android with Nano 4, or iOS with Apple Foundation Models. Firestore, Remote Config and Auth are examples, not three additional mandatory services. Pick Web/Chrome. Hybrid local/cloud is the point; no substitute model or mock.
- Repository: public GitHub or GitLab, detectable open-source license (Apache preferred), all source, assets, setup and testing instructions. Supply a working test path, no production keys/secrets/PII in repo or submission. Hosted URL strongly encouraged.
- Submission: select platform, explain features, value and AI Logic, provide repo URL and **public YouTube video**, English or English subtitles. At most three minutes are evaluated. Show the actual platform; no unauthorized trademarks, assets or endorsements.
- First gate: viability/requirements pass or fail. Then 1–5 on each, equally weighted: technological implementation, design, potential impact, quality of idea. Ties use that order then a vote. Grand prizes $10k/$7k/$4k; best iOS, Android, Web/Chrome $3k each. One grand and at most one Best Of per entry.
- No purchase necessary. Additional quota is optional, not requested. No submission, paid plan or account registration performed. Unknown: organizer acceptance of a particular device/model build, AI-assisted ownership, or submission until reviewed by organizers.

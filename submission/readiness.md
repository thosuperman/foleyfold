## Product and repository
**Foleyfold** helps independent filmmakers and caption editors describe meaningful sounds in unreleased footage. Unlike a dialogue transcriber or an automatic classifier, the human chooses the sound, time span and final wording. The media stays local; only a bounded listening note and duration can be used for AI. Human approval gates timed WebVTT export. This is a post-production tool, not a field-inspection product. Audience impact is a hypothesis, not user-study evidence.
